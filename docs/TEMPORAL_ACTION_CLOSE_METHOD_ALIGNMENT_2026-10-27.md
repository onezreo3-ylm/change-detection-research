One new search request contained three queries; three distinct primary full-text
resources were inspected, with follow-up section reads. Previously blocked STAR,
ChangeMask, AsymCD, CPEN and IEEE entries were not retried or bypassed. This is
not an exhaustive novelty review. Known TIN/SIGE normalization replay, temporal

The remaining narrow question is whether mutually exclusive placements of the
same real correction have *enough additional task benefit* to justify studying
their pre-action error-transition forecasts. First compare a truth-informed
four-action upper bound with the old two-action bound and strong fixed temporal
placements. If that space is insufficient, close it before learning. If it is
sufficient, a later protocol must isolate forecast target, decision rule and
information against matched ordinary local gating/value prediction and the
four-decoder-output postselection control. Full pipeline timing is necessary;
decoder call counts are not a latency or FLOP lower bound.
